# Reclaim Your Focus

A landing page for **Reclaim Your Focus: A 21-Day Productivity Reset for Young Africans**, a practical digital field manual designed to help young Africans overcome distractions, build better habits and take control of their time.

## About the Project

Reclaim Your Focus is a digital product landing page built to introduce the productivity guide, communicate its value and encourage visitors to purchase the PDF.

The page is designed around a simple idea: productivity isn't just about motivation; it's about building systems that make it easier to focus, stay consistent and get meaningful work done.

## Key Features

- Product introduction and overview.
- Clear presentation of the 21-day programme.
- Explanation of the problems the guide addresses.
- Product benefits and key features.
- Purchase calls-to-action linking to Selar.
- Responsive layout for different screen sizes.
